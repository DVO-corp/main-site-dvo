# DVO Corp — passive lead channels (register once, leads come to Telegram / site)

Researched 2026-11-21/22 by web search. Scope: Ukraine first, then MD / PL / LT / LV / EE / CZ / RO / wider EU.
Hard exclusion applied: no Russian or Belarusian platforms, owners, or targeting.

How to read this file:

- **Source** links sit next to every claim about requirements or pricing. Anything without a source, or taken
  from a third-party blog instead of the platform itself, is marked **(unverified)**.
- **Effort** figures are estimates for the owner's own time with profile texts already written by someone else.
  They are estimates, not measured.
- Ranking = expected inbound leads per hour of owner effort for a 2-person DevOps team with a ФОП, not
  platform size.
- Several platform pages returned HTTP 403 to automated reading (TechBehemoths, GoodFirms help, Freelancehunt
  price page, dev.ua, telemetr.io). For those, the facts below come from search-result excerpts of the same
  pages and are flagged where that matters. See section 4.
- Lead destination everywhere: `https://t.me/dvocorp` first, `https://dvocorp.com` second. Use
  `info@dvocorp.com` as the account e-mail and the company phone `+380 63 932 08 74`; no personal contacts.
