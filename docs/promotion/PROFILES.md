form allows. Rules that keep them honest and consistent with the site:

- No client names, project counts, years of experience or savings percentages
  until they are real and can be shown.
- Contacts are always the company ones: Telegram `@dvocorp`,
  `info@dvocorp.com`, `support@dvocorp.com`, `+380 63 932 08 74`,
  site `https://dvocorp.com`.
- Never list or target Russian or Belarusian platforms or markets.
- Link landings, not just the home page — each profile field that accepts a
  URL per service should point at the matching `/services/<slug>/` page.
